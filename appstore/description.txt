mynote keeps one note in front of you, on your Lock Screen and on your Home Screen.

WRITE IT YOUR WAY
• One focused note card. No folders, no clutter.
• Rich Text editing: formatting shows as you type. The bar above the keyboard adds checkboxes, bullets, numbered lists, headings, bold, italic, strikethrough, highlights, code, quotes and dividers.
• Prefer Markdown? Switch to the Markdown editor in Settings and type **bold**, # headings and - [ ] tasks yourself. Both edit the same note.
• Lists continue when you press return, and nest when you indent them.
• A 280-character limit keeps it focused. Formatting symbols don't count.

ALWAYS ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
• Tap Go Live and your note appears on the Lock Screen, formatting and all.
• Tick items off right from the Lock Screen, no need to open the app.
• iOS ends each Live Activity after 8 hours. mynote's Auto-Refresh lines up fresh ones back to back, keeping your list in view for about a day and a half without opening the app.
• One-tap refresh from Control Center, the Lock Screen controls, a widget or a Shortcuts automation.
• An optional reminder before your list would disappear.

MAKE IT YOURS
• 50 colour themes, from deep darks to soft pastels.
• Tap the palette to try any theme on for free. Default Light and Default Dark are free; Supporters get all 50. No subscription.
• Pair a light and a dark theme that switch with your iPhone, or keep one theme all the time.
• Your theme carries through to the widget and the Live Activity.
• 10 fonts, from clean and rounded to serif and typewriter.

SUPPORT AN INDIE APP
• mynote is made by one person. Become a mynote Supporter with a one-time purchase: it unlocks all 50 themes for good and helps keep mynote growing.

BUILT FOR iOS 26
• A Liquid Glass design that feels at home on iPhone.
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: tick a box right on the widget, or tap anywhere else to open mynote.
• Siri and Shortcuts: add items, clear completed items, keep your list on the Lock Screen.
• Deleted notes wait in the Archive until you delete them for good, with search and one-tap restore.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your note stays on your iPhone.
