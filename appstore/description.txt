mynote keeps one list in front of you: on your Lock Screen, in the Dynamic Island and on your Home Screen. The things that matter today never get buried.

WRITE ONE CLEAR LIST
• A single note card. No folders, no clutter.
• Type [ ] for a checkbox, - for a bullet and # for a heading, or use the buttons above the keyboard.
• Lists continue automatically when you press return.
• A 280-character limit keeps it focused. List markers don't count.

ALWAYS ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
• Tap Go Live and your list appears on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
• Tick items off right from the Lock Screen, no need to open the app.
• iOS ends each Live Activity after 8 hours. mynote's Auto-Refresh lines up fresh ones back to back, keeping your list in view for about a day and a half without opening the app.
• One-tap refresh from Control Center, the Lock Screen controls, a widget or a Shortcuts automation.
• An optional reminder before your list would disappear.

THEMES DEVS LOVE
• 50 colour themes inspired by popular code editor and typing themes, from dark classics to soft pastels.
• Pair a light and a dark theme that switch with your iPhone, or keep one theme all the time.
• Your theme carries through to the widget and the Live Activity.
• 10 fonts including SF Mono, Menlo and Courier.

BUILT FOR iOS 26
• A Liquid Glass design that feels at home on iPhone.
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets with tappable checkboxes.
• Siri and Shortcuts: add items, clear completed items, keep your list on the Lock Screen.
• Archive notes and bring them back any time, with search.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no tracking, no analytics. Your note stays on your iPhone.
