Frequently asked questions

How do I make a checkbox or a bullet?

Start a line with [ ] for a checkbox or - for a bullet, or use the two buttons above the keyboard while editing. Press return to continue the list; press return on an empty item to end it. # at the start of a line makes a heading.

How do I put my list on the Lock Screen?

Tap Go Live at the bottom of the main screen. Your note appears on the Lock Screen, and you can tick checkboxes right there. Tap Live again to remove it.

Why did my list disappear from the Lock Screen?

iOS limits every Live Activity to 8 hours. With Auto-Refresh on (Settings → Live Activity), mynote schedules fresh Live Activities to take over just before each one ends, which covers about a day and a half. Opening mynote tops the chain back up. If it ever runs out, mynote sends a reminder notification. Tap it and your list is back.

Your list can also disappear if Live Activities are turned off for mynote in the iOS Settings app (Settings → mynote → Live Activities), or if you swipe it away on the Lock Screen.

Can my iPhone refresh the list automatically?
  1. Open the Shortcuts app → Automation → New Automation.
  2. Choose Time of Day (for example 7:00 AM, daily) or Charger → Is Connected.
  3. Select Run Immediately, then tap Next.
  4. Add the action Keep mynote on Lock Screen and tap Done.

You can also add the Refresh mynote control to Control Center or to your Lock Screen for one-tap refreshes.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, search for mynote and choose a size. Checkboxes on the widget can be ticked without opening the app. On the Lock Screen, touch and hold → Customize → Lock Screen to add the small mynote widgets.

How do I change the theme or font?

Tap ⋯ → Settings → Theme. Pick any of the 50 themes, or turn on Match System Appearance to choose one theme for light mode and one for dark mode. Fonts and text size live under Settings → Note Text.

Why is there a character limit?

mynote is built for one clear list, not a long messy note, and the Lock Screen only has room for so much. Notes can hold 280 characters. Checkbox and bullet markers don't count.

Where are my notes stored? Can I get them back?

Everything stays on your iPhone. Archived notes are kept under ⋯ → Archived Notes, where you can search and restore them. Deleted notes cannot be recovered, and deleting the app removes all of its notes.
